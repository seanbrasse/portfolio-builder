This font is bundled here as a placeholder display face. To swap in a
licensed comic-lettering face (Blambot or otherwise), drop the woff2 files
into this directory and update the single @font-face block plus the
`--font-display` custom property in src/app/globals.css. Nothing else in the
codebase names a font family.
