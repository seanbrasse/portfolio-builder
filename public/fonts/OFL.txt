Bundled here as a placeholder display face, subset to the glyphs this site
sets. To swap in a licensed comic-lettering face (Blambot or otherwise),
replace bangers.woff2 here and src/assets/bangers.ttf (the OG image generator
rasterises through Satori, which cannot read woff2), then update the single
@font-face block in src/app/globals.css. Nothing else in the codebase names a
font family.
