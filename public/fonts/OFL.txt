Subset to the glyphs this site sets. To swap in a licensed comic-lettering
face, replace display.woff2 here and src/assets/display.ttf (the OG image
generator rasterises through Satori, which cannot read woff2), then update the
single @font-face block in src/app/globals.css. Nothing else in the codebase
names a font family.
